## What to do
### `POST /v1/charges` (endpoint-deprecated)

Stripe Charges API is deprecated. Migrating to PaymentIntents changes the payment flow (create → attach method → confirm → SCA) and cannot safely preserve immediate charge-with-source behavior.

**Before:** `stripe.charges.create({ amount, currency, source })`  
**After (guidance only):** `Manual: paymentIntents.create + payment_method/source mapping + confirm (+ 3DS). Do not drop `source` or invent emails.`

**Why automation stopped:**
- Charges with `source`/`tok_*` immediately captures payment. PaymentIntents usually only *create* an intent until confirm.
- Do not replace charges.create with paymentIntents.create alone — that drops the payment method and changes success semantics.
- Do not invent customer emails or rename response keys (`charge` → `paymentIntent`) without an intentional API version bump.
- Preserve route contracts (`/legacy-charge` returning `{ charge }`) unless you also update callers.
- Safe automation: flag for human review. Unsafe automation: silent incomplete PaymentIntent create.
